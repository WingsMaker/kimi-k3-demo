Info	**Translation:**

"When the sun was setting on me, you weren't by my side; now that I'm rising again, who do you think you are?"

**More natural English versions:**

- "You didn't stand by me when I was down, so who are you now that I'm back on top?"
- "You weren't there during my hard times — you have no place in my success."

**Explanation:**

This is a popular Chinese saying about fair-weather friends. It uses two idioms:

- **日落西山** (the sun setting in the west) = being at rock bottom, in decline
- **东山再起** (rising again from the eastern mountain) = making a comeback

The phrase expresses resentment toward people who abandon you during difficult times but try to come back once you've succeeded. It's essentially saying: *"If you couldn't support me at my worst, you don't deserve me at my best."*
